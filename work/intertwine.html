Case study · iOS app & website · 2026

Intertwine

One wedding, in one place. An iPhone app where couples plan events, guests and a registry, and a website where each guest opens a personal invite link: no app or account needed.

My role

Research, UX/UI, brand, and building the app and website

Timeline

Fall 2026

Course

Everyday Enhancements, Module 3 · MassArt

Tools

Figma, SwiftUI, HTML/CSS/JS, Supabase

The problem

Wedding details live everywhere: texts, spreadsheets, websites and registries.

Couples answer the same guest questions over and over, and guests dig through group chats for times and dress codes. Intertwine gives the couple one place to plan, and gives every guest one link with everything they need.

For the couple

iPhone app

For guests

Website · personal invite link

Process

From site map to a working app.

Step 1

Research

How couples and guests share wedding info today.

Step 2

Site map

Every screen for both the couple and the guest.

Step 3

Brand pass

A script monogram, warm neutrals and Alegreya Sans headings.

Step 4

Build

A SwiftUI iPhone app and a guest website, connected by Supabase.

Step 5

Test

A real wedding, real invites and RSVPs, then fix what broke.

Outcome

One wedding, in one place.

The app and website are live and connected. Guests RSVP, ask questions and claim gifts on the website, and it all shows up in the couple's app.
