Intertwine

One wedding, in one place. An iPhone app where couples plan events, guests and a registry, and a website where each guest opens a personal invite link: no app or account needed.

My role

Solo: research, UX/UI, brand, and building the app and website

Status

In progress · Fall 2026

Course

Everyday Enhancements, Module 3 · MassArt

Tools

Figma, SwiftUI, HTML/CSS/JS, Supabase

The problem

Wedding details live everywhere: texts, spreadsheets, websites and registries.

Couples answer the same guest questions over and over, and guests dig through group chats for times, places and dress codes. Most wedding apps make guests download something or create an account first.

Intertwine splits the job in two: the couple gets an iPhone app to plan, and every guest gets one personal link that just works in their browser.

Mapping two journeys

Site map

I mapped every screen for both sides: the couple setting up a wedding and the guest opening an invite. Onboarding alone became a 12-step flow, from choosing a role to publishing the first event.
