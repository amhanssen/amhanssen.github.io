Case study · Typography installation · 2026

Splash Zone

Vinyl type in a campus bathroom that turns an everyday room into a playful, slightly uncomfortable experience.

My role

Team project: concept, mockups and installation

Timeline

4 weeks · Spring 2026

Course

Typography III, with Katherine Hughes

Tools

Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, Procreate

The idea

What happens to type when you can walk into it?

Our team walked around campus looking for places with a strong mood or an interesting way people use them. The bathroom won: an everyday, private space where letters splashing across the tiles feel funny and a little too close.

Three concepts, one pick

Week 1

Chosen

Splash Zone

Water-drop letters spreading across a bathroom's walls.

Concept 2

Don't Spiral

A circular window piece about anxiety.

Concept 3

Sunshine

A warmer installation for a student lounge.

Process

From mood board to tile wall.

Week 1

Research

Scouting spaces, three concepts, mood boards and mockups.

Week 2

Test

Full-scale mockups to test placement, scale and vinyl.

Week 4

Install

The final vinyl installation in the bathroom.

Outcome

“It really shifted how I think about type.”

Type became something to experience, not just read. Splash Zone was chosen for its playful tone, its tie to the space and the way it surrounds you.
