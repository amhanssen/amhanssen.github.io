Case study · App concept · 2025

DreamCast

A Headspace feature that helps people fall asleep, remember their dreams and explore their subconscious in a mindful way.

My role

Personas, user stories, wireframes, journey map, storyboards, interface and video

Timeline

7 weeks · Fall 2025

Course

Information Architecture, with Martha Rettig

Tools

Figma, After Effects, Google Suite

The idea

What if your sleep app helped you remember your dreams, too?

Headspace already helps people wind down. DreamCast adds a bedtime story that eases you to sleep and a gentle morning log, so you can capture a dream before it fades and get suggestions based on what you remember.

Two personas, one happy path

Who it’s for

College student

Flora

Balancing school stress and sleep. Wants to fall asleep faster and remember her dreams.

Fairy guardian of the Dream Portal

Maya

The playful guide who greets Flora each night and carries her into the story.

Process

Seven weeks, from a dream to a video.

Weeks 2–3

Concept

Settled on dreams and wrote the personas.

Week 4

Journey map

Mapped the happy path and cut features down to Flora’s needs.

Week 5

Storyboards

Script, storyboards and a second interface draft that fits Headspace.

Week 6

First video

Bedtime flow: notification, story, dreaming, morning log.

Week 7

Polish

Pacing, color and readability, checked against Flora’s goals.

Outcome

“Refining ideas through research and iteration made something more focused and meaningful.”

The final pieces were a polished interface and a video of the full bedtime flow. The biggest lesson: balancing creativity with simplicity, on a deadline.
