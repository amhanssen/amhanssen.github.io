About

Hi, I'm Amanda.

I'm a Communication Design student at MassArt, splitting my time between Boston and Laconia, NH. I care about small, everyday moments: a menu, a sign on a lake, a poster on a wall. I sketch in Procreate, build in Figma and bring things to life in After Effects.
